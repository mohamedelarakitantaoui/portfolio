# Shre7lia: Fusion AI Hackathon 2026 (2nd place)

Verified facts. Mamoun confirmed them on 15 Oct 2026; they come from the team's own post and Aya Dafir's LinkedIn post.
Use only these. Leave out anything not listed here.

- **Event:** AUI AI Summer School & Fusion AI Hackathon 2026, at Al Akhawayn University, Ifrane.
  - Six days, ending around 17 July 2026.
  - Organised by Al Akhawayn University, Afretec Network and ABA Technology.
- **Result:** 2nd place.
- **Team:** 6 people: Aya Dafir, Aya Abi, Saad Boualam, Mohamed Al Mamoun Tantaoui El Araki, Abdelkhalek Ouaraqy,
  Meryem Ghanem.
  - On the site, say "team of 6". Don't list the teammates' full names unless Mamoun asks.
- **Project:** Shre7lia, an AI study assistant that turns course materials into instant, grounded answers,
  available to students 24/7.
- **Problem:**
  - Students lose hours every week searching PDFs and slides for answers.
  - Professors aren't always reachable.
  - Generic AI tools hallucinate or drift away from the course material.
  - Universities struggle to support students at scale.
- **How it was built:**
  - Automation workflows end-to-end, from document ingestion to answer generation, on ABA Technology's
    Fusion AI platform.
  - Each team member built one workflow.
  - Retrieval: hybrid RAG (not fine-tuning).
- **Safety:** secured against prompt injection and data leaks as part of the architecture.
- **Research:** the team surveyed students at both public and private universities before building.
- **For professors:**
  - They upload their courses, see insights on every student, and are notified when a student starts lagging
    behind or a piece of course information is missing.
  - Every session ends with a mini quiz, and the results feed the professor insights.
- **Mamoun's role:** team member; he built one of the Fusion AI workflows.
