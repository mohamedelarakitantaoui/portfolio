# research.md: Stage 2 niche research (Mode P)

Niche: junior AI / data / software engineer portfolio that doubles as a freelance shop
(automation, scraping, private RAG, web MVPs). Readers: recruiters (Morocco, remote,
EU/US/Canada from early 2027) and small clients.

Queries run: `awwwards portfolio developer site of the day 2026`,
`software engineer portfolio must-have features recruiters 2026`,
`freelance developer automation scraping services productized packages pricing`.
Sites studied in a browser: see references.md (5 sites, 9 pages).

## What recruiters look for (from the search results)
- 3-5 strong projects beat many weak ones; recruiters skim in a limited window
  ([Proxify](https://proxify.io/knowledge-base/job-descriptions/what-makes-a-developer-portfolio-stand-out-to-recruiters),
  [8seneca](https://www.8seneca.com/de/blog/technology/software-engineer-portfolio-guide-what-to-include-and-what-to-avoid)).
- Impact numbers: performance gains, users, cost or time saved
  ([Fullscale](https://fullscale.io/blog/ways-to-enhance-your-software-engineer-portfolio)).
- Real problems over tutorial projects; a clear niche
  ([DesignGurus](https://www.designgurus.io/answers/detail/how-should-a-software-engineer-portfolio-look-like)).
- Fast load, clear buttons.

## What freelance clients see elsewhere (market context, not for the site)
- Scraping gigs are sold as 3 tiers (Starter / Standard / Advanced). One Upwork example
  ran $100 / $200 / $500 with 2 / 3 / 5-day delivery; experienced scrapers charge about
  $30-60/h ([Upwork listing](https://www.upwork.com/services/product/development-it-custom-efficient-web-scraping-services-for-your-data-extraction-needs-1657398502044454912),
  [Fiverr](https://de.fiverr.com/pivot_metric/scrape-website-data-using-python-and-selenium)).
- So the packs need scope, deliverable, timeline and a "from" price to compete with
  marketplace listings. Prices stay `TODO(user)`.

## Consensus sitemap
Mandatory (≥50% or required by the brief): Home, Work (index), Case study pages, About
(experience), CV, Contact.
Brief-required differentiators: Services, Creative, FR mirror, Cmd+K, terminal.
Skipped: blog/insights (no content; would become a "coming soon").
Added by the skill's footer rule: `/privacy` (short; there are no cookies and no tracking) and a 404.
