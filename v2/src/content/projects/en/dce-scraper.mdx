<Chapter n="01" label="Problem" title="A chatbot needed tender documents. The portals hand them out one form at a time.">

Intelcia needed a dataset of public-procurement documents to train an AI chatbot. Those documents are the **DCE** (dossier de consultation des entreprises), the tender file attached to each consultation. They sit behind consultation pages and download forms on three sources: Morocco's public-procurement portal **marchespublics.gov.ma**, **marocao.com**, and Intelcia's internal purchasing portal.

The first version of the collector was one 3,643-line script. A run over 300 consultations took 2.5 hours, and when a download failed, nothing said so: the file was simply missing from the dataset.

</Chapter>

<PullQuote cite="Title of my internship report, Intelcia, summer 2025">Automated Data Collection for AI Chatbot Training: Development of a DCE Web Scraping System</PullQuote>

<Chapter n="02" label="Approach" title="Split it by job, then run ten browsers at once.">

- **One responsibility per service.** The monolith became seven services, each owning one step: find the URLs, fill the forms, download, file, retry.
- **A worker pool.** Up to 10 Selenium browsers run in parallel, scaled by configuration.
- **Config, not code, per portal.** Each source is described in YAML, so a new portal or a changed selector is a config edit.
- **Loud failure.** Retries look at the failure pattern before trying again, and health checks flag anything that still fails.

</Chapter>
