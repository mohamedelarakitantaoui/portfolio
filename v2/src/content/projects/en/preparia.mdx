<Chapter n="01" label="Problem" title="Exam prep students practise on, not only read.">

ITS Elaraki School, in Marrakech, needed an online platform to prepare its students for the baccalaureate, in French, across 6 subjects.

</Chapter>

<Chapter n="02" label="Constraints" title="One summer, on WordPress, in French.">

- **Time:** one summer internship, June to August 2024.
- **Stack:** WordPress and Elementor, with H5P for the interactive content.
- **Language:** the platform shipped in French, the language its students read.
- **Adoption:** students and teachers had to use it on their own, without follow-up IT support.

</Chapter>
