<Chapter n="01" label="Problem" title="Answers buried in PDFs and slides.">

Students lose hours searching PDFs and slides. Professors are not always reachable, and generic AI tools can drift away from course material. Universities struggle to support students at scale. Before building, the team surveyed students at public and private universities.

</Chapter>

<Chapter n="02" label="What we built" title="An assistant grounded in university courses.">

Our team of 6 built Shre7lia, an AI study assistant that turns course materials into instant, grounded answers, available 24/7. We took 2nd place at Fusion AI Hackathon 2026 at Al Akhawayn University. My role was team member; I built one of the Fusion AI workflows.

</Chapter>

<Chapter n="03" label="How it works" title="Fusion AI workflows and hybrid RAG.">

End-to-end automation workflows on ABA Technology’s Fusion AI platform connect document ingestion to answer generation. Each team member built one workflow. Retrieval uses hybrid RAG. Protection against prompt injection and data leaks is part of the architecture.

</Chapter>

<Chapter n="04" label="For professors" title="Course uploads, quizzes and student insights.">

Professors upload courses and see insights on every student. They are notified when a student starts lagging behind or course information is missing. Every session ends with a mini quiz whose results feed these insights.

</Chapter>
